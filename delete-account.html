Request account or cloud-data deletion

If you use a skorpion.tech app that provides an optional signed-in/cloud account, you can request deletion of that account and its associated cloud data.

Email skorpion.tech to request account/data deletion

Please include:

  • the app name;
  • the Google/email account used to sign in, where applicable;
  • a short statement requesting deletion of your account and associated cloud data.

We may ask for limited information needed to verify that the request relates to the correct account.

Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run

Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run can be played as a guest. Signed-in users may use Google Sign-In and Firebase-based cloud features so game progress can be synchronized across devices.

In-app deletion

Where the signed-in account feature is enabled, the app provides an account/data deletion option from the account or profile area. This removes the app's cloud account data associated with the signed-in user.

Web request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can use the email link above to request deletion.

Data intended to be deleted

  • cloud-synchronized campaign and level progress;
  • stars, streaks and achievements stored in the cloud;
  • cloud-stored coins and unlocked game items;
  • cloud profile/game-handle information;
  • saved active-game state used for cross-device resume;
  • Firebase account-linked game data controlled by skorpion.tech.

Data held independently by Google, such as Google Play purchase records, advertising records or security/fraud-prevention records, may be retained by Google under its own policies and legal obligations.

Games Arena: Ludo & More

If a version of Games Arena provides a signed-in/cloud account, online profile or cloud synchronization, you may request deletion through the in-app account controls where available or through the email request link above.

Deletion applies to account-associated cloud data controlled by skorpion.tech. Local-only game data can also be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Pocket Aptitude: Offline

Pocket Aptitude currently does not require a cloud account for its core learning features. Learning progress, bookmarks and similar study data are stored locally on the device.

You can remove local Pocket Aptitude data by using any available reset option in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

If a future version adds a signed-in/cloud account, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

What deletion does not automatically remove

Deleting a skorpion.tech app account does not necessarily delete information maintained independently by third-party services where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction or accounting purposes.

For example, Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records under Google's own policies. Google advertising services may also retain information according to Google's applicable privacy and retention practices.

Local data

Cloud-account deletion does not automatically erase files or preferences already stored only on your device. To remove local app data, you can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
