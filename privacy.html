1. Scope and overview

This Privacy Policy explains how skorpion.tech mobile applications handle information. The exact features used differ by app.

  • Pocket Aptitude: Offline is an aptitude-learning app with theory, solved examples, practice, tests, bookmarks, mistakes review and locally stored learning progress. It may display advertising when online.
  • Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run is a puzzle game that can be played as a guest and may offer optional Google sign-in, Firebase cloud save/sync, cross-device progress, global leaderboard features, advertising, and Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Games Arena: Ludo & More is a collection of board and casual games. It may use online/friend-game services where enabled, display advertising, and offer Google Play purchases such as coins, themes or a one-time Remove Ads option.

Core offline features are designed to remain usable without requiring a social-media account or separate third-party payment gateway.

2. Information stored locally on your device

Depending on the app and features you use, information stored locally may include:

  • game or learning progress, completed levels/topics and scores;
  • stars, streaks, achievements, rankings, coins and unlockable items;
  • bookmarks, saved questions, mistakes or review items;
  • current or paused game state so an unfinished session can be resumed;
  • player name, selected avatar and locally chosen profile image;
  • app preferences such as theme, sound, haptics, display settings and similar choices;
  • purchase-entitlement state such as whether Remove Ads is owned, where applicable.

Local information is used to provide the requested app features. Clearing app data or uninstalling an app may remove locally stored information unless that information has been synchronized to an optional cloud account.

3. Accounts, Firebase and cloud features

Some apps may provide optional online or cloud features using Google Firebase services.

Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run

Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run may be used as a guest without Google sign-in. If you choose Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication may process an account identifier and basic Google account information made available through the sign-in process. Firebase Realtime Database may then be used to synchronize game progress and related data across devices.

Cloud-synchronized information may include campaign progress, level results, stars, streaks, achievements, coins, unlocked avatars/themes, selected game profile information, leaderboard data and a saved active-game snapshot used for cross-device resume.

The public leaderboard should use a game handle/avatar rather than automatically publishing your Google email address or private Google account details.

Games Arena: Ludo & More

Where online matchmaking, friend rooms or similar connected-game features are enabled, Games Arena may use Firebase or related Google services to exchange the minimum identifiers and game-state information required to provide those features.

Pocket Aptitude: Offline

Pocket Aptitude currently does not require a user account for its core learning features. Learning progress, bookmarks and similar study information are intended to remain stored locally unless a future version clearly introduces an optional cloud feature and this policy is updated accordingly.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's own privacy and security practices.

  • Pocket Aptitude: Offline: may use banner and interstitial advertising.
  • Arrow Puzzle: Escape Run: may use interstitial and optional rewarded advertising, such as voluntary ads for coins, hints or revives.
  • Games Arena: Ludo & More: may use banner and interstitial advertising, and may use rewarded advertising if such a feature is introduced.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK documentation, advertising services may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general/approximate location;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID where applicable.

This information may be processed by Google for purposes including advertising, analytics, ad measurement, service delivery, security and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by Google advertising services is handled according to Google's applicable policies.
